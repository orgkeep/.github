# Security policy

This is the default policy of the repositories of Orgkeep that have none of their own.

Never report a vulnerability in a public issue or pull request. Use
[*Report a vulnerability*](https://github.com/orgkeep/orgkeep/security/advisories/new) in
[orgkeep](https://github.com/orgkeep/orgkeep): the report stays private between you and the maintainer. Its
[security policy](https://github.com/orgkeep/orgkeep/blob/main/SECURITY.md) says what to include and what to
expect.
